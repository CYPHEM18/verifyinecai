Summary of INEC's public notice of 3 October 2026, posted on INEC's official X account.

INEC named pvc-inecnigeria-org.blogspot.com as a fake website with no affiliation to the Commission.

The fake website falsely announced a nationwide online voter registration window from 28 September to 27 October 2026.

INEC said the voter registration exercise closed on 26 July 2026, and that any information saying a new nationwide online voter registration exercise is ongoing through that website is false.

INEC advised the public not to click on the link or submit personal information on the website, because personal data could be harvested or misused.

INEC urged the public to remain vigilant and to verify electoral information through the Commission's official communication channels.
